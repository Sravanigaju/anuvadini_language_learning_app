Awesome Features

LrN is a part of the Anuvadini-AI project under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, developed to preserve and promote India’s rich linguistic heritage through innovative and inclusive technology.

Gamified Learning

Compete on leaderboards, earn coins, complete challenges, and play games designed to make language learning enjoyable and rewarding.

Multilingual Support

Access learning and translation capabilities in any of India’s 23 official languages—including Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Gujarati, Sanskrit, Bodo, and others—using the source language of your choice.

Inclusive for All

Designed for learners of all literacy levels, age groups, regions, and backgrounds across India.

User-Friendly Interface

Clean, interactive design with intuitive navigation and guided workflows to support seamless learning.

Voice-Guided Accessibility

Every form field and slide is narrated with text-to-speech (TTS) and accepts responses via speech-to-text (STT), enabling fully interactive, hands-free operation.

Personalized Learning

Lessons adapt to the learner’s current language exposure and skill level for a personalized experience.

Assessment and Certification

Complete stages and courses to earn verifiable digital certificates — useful for showcasing your language proficiency.

Multiplayer Challenges

Group-based games and learning challenges foster collaboration and healthy competition among users.

Audio-Visual Support

Each slide integrates pronunciation audio, contextual imagery, and authentic usage examples to reinforce understanding and retention.

Structured Curriculum

Lessons follow a level-wise progression: Basic → Intermediate → Advanced, organized into lessons, chapters, and slides.

Progress Tracking and Rewards

Track daily goals, unlock levels, maintain learning streaks, earn XP points, and receive achievement certificates.

Awesome Services

Blending Structure, Interactivity, and Personalization for Powerful Language Learning

Guided Language Courses

Structured modules for all levels covering vocabulary, grammar, conversations, alphabets, comprehension, and more.

Certificate-Based Assessments

Language proficiency evaluations with downloadable certificates recognized by educational and institutional bodies.

Progress Tracking & Analytics

Monitor learning activity, performance, streaks, scores, and completion status in real time.

Localized Game-Based Learning Modules

Regionally contextual games for alphabets, numbers, greetings, daily life terms, and cultural expressions.

Data-Private Hosting and Storage

All user data is securely stored on Indian servers with zero involvement from thirdparty platforms.
